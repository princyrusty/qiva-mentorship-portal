What is mentorship

A mentor is someone a few steps ahead who walks with you

A teacher covers a syllabus. A mentor works on you: your goal, your habits, your doubts and the next step that will actually move you forward.

It can be a student stuck on a chapter, a graduate choosing a first job, a founder with a half-formed idea, or anyone who wants to speak, think or learn with more confidence. At QIVA each session is one to one, in a private video room, at a time that suits you.

Most people do not fail for lack of effort. They lose months going in the wrong direction, doubting themselves, or waiting for an answer nobody gave. A mentor shortens that road.

Why a mentor beats another video

It starts at your exact doubt

A course moves at the speed of its syllabus. A session begins at the line where you got stuck.

You can interrupt

Stop the explanation, ask the question, and get an answer in the context of your own problem.

The mentor looks at your attempt and points to the step where it went wrong.

You pay for the topic

No bundle to finish. Buy 30 minutes for one concept, or a month when you need a routine.

Why subscribe instead of booking one at a time?

Doubts rarely come alone. A weekly slot turns scattered questions into a habit, keeps one mentor who already knows where you struggle, and costs less per session. The calculator above shows the exact rate for every option.
